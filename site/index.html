Linux · macOS · Windows · open source

Every Guitar Pro file

GP3, GP4 and GP5, plus the newer .gpx and .gp formats. Open them from the picker or drop them on the window.

Sound in the binary

A soundfont ships inside the executable, so playback works on a fresh machine. Point it at a bigger one when you want better tone.

Built for practice

Slow a passage to a quarter speed, solo the track you are learning, and let repeats and alternate endings play out on their own.

What it does

  • GP3-GP7 file support
  • MIDI playback embedded soundfont
  • 25-200% tempo control
  • Solo mode one track at a time
  • Repeats with alternate endings
  • Track picker per file
  • Light and dark themes
  • Drag and drop to open
